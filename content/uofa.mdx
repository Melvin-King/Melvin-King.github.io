I'm thrilled to join the Computing Science Department at the University of Alberta as an international [Amii](https://www.amii.ca/) PhD student! Supervised by [Prof. Yang](https://homepage.zhouyang.me/) in the [U-A-Goose Lab](https://u-a-goose.github.io/), I will focus my research on trustworthy software engineering for the AI era (e.g., large language models for code, AI security and privacy, and efficient and sustainable AI systems). In addition to advancing my research, I'll be working as a teaching assistant for some undergraduate courses in my first year.

Looking forward to connecting with the academic community here and embracing all the challenges ahead!
